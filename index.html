    What is Brewers Cup?

    The Brewers Cup is a platform where coffee professionals come together to showcase the art and science of manual brewing. Competitors present a coffee of their choice, carefully crafting every variable — from grind size and water to pouring technique and extraction — to bring out its finest qualities.

    But it's about more than just brewing a great cup. It's about understanding the coffee, communicating its story, and creating an experience that connects the judges to what's in the cup.

    At the Mumbai Regionals, our approach, our coffee, and our story came together to earn us the win.

    About the Coffee

    A blend of two remarkable coffees — Four Strain Yeast and Excelsa — crafted to bring together sweetness, bright acidity, body, and complexity.

    Four strain yeast is a Selection 13, also known as Chandragiri. Fermented inside a tank using a mixed culture of four carefully selected yeast strains for sweetness and complexity. The cherries are fermented with these yeasts for 60 hours by a method called carbonic maceration, which influences the cup into becoming vivid, fruity, and aromatic.

    And then Excelsa, known for its naturally low acidity and fuller body, brings a weight and depth the Arabica alone doesn't have. The coffee cherries were fermented for 60 hours as well by the same process of carbonic maceration.

    Expect an expressive cup with notes of plum, apricot, red grapes, and sweet resins, complemented by a juicy, rounded mouthfeel and a lingering finish.

    This is the coffee we brewed to win the National Brewers Cup Mumbai Regionals. Now, we're bringing that same experience to your cup.

    About the Estate

    Nestled in Nakoor village in North Coorg, Karnataka, Mooleh Manay Estate is a family-run coffee farm with roots dating back to the early 1800s. Today, it is led by Akshay Dashrath and Komal Sable, who also co-founded South India Coffee Company (SICC), working at the intersection of coffee cultivation, processing, and research.

    What makes the estate special is its commitment to exploring coffee beyond conventional boundaries, growing Arabica, Robusta, and the relatively uncommon Excelsa. Through experimental fermentation and a research-driven approach to processing, Mooleh Manay continues to explore new possibilities in Indian coffee.
