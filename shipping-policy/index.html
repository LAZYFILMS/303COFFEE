Shipping policy

Updated October 23, 2026

Shipping is free. 303 Coffee ships the product to the delivery address entered at checkout, and no shipping charge is added.

  • Shipping is free on every order.
  • You are responsible for a complete and accurate shipping address. An order sent to the address you enter is treated as delivered to you.
  • Orders are prepared for shipment after payment is received. A delivery date is not guaranteed. Delivery time depends on the destination and the carrier.
  • After an order is placed, it cannot be cancelled, changed, or redirected.
  • Risk of loss passes when the order is handed to the carrier. A delay, a carrier problem, customs, or an incorrect address does not create a refund.

Questions about a shipment can be sent from the contact page. A message does not change this policy.
